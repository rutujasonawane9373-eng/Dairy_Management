About Dairy

Dairy farming is an important part of rural India's economy. Small farmers depend on daily milk collection for a steady income. A dairy cooperative society collects milk from farmers every morning and evening, tests its quality, and pays the farmers based on the quantity and quality of milk.

Our Dairy Management System makes this process digital. It records every farmer, every milk collection, the fat and SNF value of each sample, and generates the payment and reports automatically.

How Milk Collection Works

Every day, the society follows this simple process:

  1. Farmer brings the milk to the collection centre in a can.
  2. The milk is weighed and the quantity (litres) is recorded.
  3. The milk sample is checked for fat and SNF percentages.
  4. The rate (Rs per litre) is decided based on fat content.
  5. Amount = Milk Quantity x Rate is calculated.
  6. Every fortnight, the total payment is given to the farmer.

Services for Farmers

Registered farmers get the following services:

  • Daily milk pickup at the collection centre
  • Fat and SNF quality testing for every collection
  • Fortnightly payment based on fat and quantity
  • Free cattle feed advisory and veterinary support
  • Monthly milk production report for every farmer
